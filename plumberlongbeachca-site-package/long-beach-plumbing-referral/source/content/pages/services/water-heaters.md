## Water Heaters - Standard {#water-heaters-standard}

A standard heater in this guide means a storage tank: a vessel that holds heated water and refills as you use it. Tanks differ by fuel, volume, vent, and where they sit. A garage closet, an exterior enclosure, and an interior hall are not the same access problem. Seismic strapping, a drain pan, and the combustion air for a gas tank are items a provider looks at. This page does not certify that your tank has them.

Sediment and scale are maintenance questions, not a slogan. Because the local supply is moderately hard to hard, minerals are a fair topic to raise with the provider. They are not a measured grain count, and they are not proof the tank must be replaced. Ask what service would include and what it would not.

## Water Heater - Repair or Service {#water-heater-repair-or-service}

Repair starts with a symptom that can be checked: no hot water, water that runs out quickly, a wet pan, a noisy tank, or a pilot or igniter that will not stay lit. Some of those are parts. Some are the end of the tank. A provider who has not seen the unit should not be quoted on this website as already knowing which.

If you are a Long Beach gas customer and there is no gas odor, the utility tells you to call its gas line at [(562) 570-2100](tel:+15625702100), or [(562) 570-2140](tel:+15625702140) after hours, because the utility supplies the gas and only cold water. If the heater is electric, the same city page points electric customers to Southern California Edison at [(800) 655-4555](tel:+18006554555). Those are utility directions published by Long Beach Utilities. They are not this referral number. After fuel is confirmed, a plumber may still be the right next call for the appliance itself.

Service, as opposed to a breakdown visit, means flushing, checking the anode where the design allows, and looking at vents and connections. Whether a referred provider offers a maintenance visit is unconfirmed. Ask for the list of tasks rather than the word “tune-up.”

## Water Heater - Install or Replace {#water-heater-install-or-replace}

Replacement is reasonable when the tank leaks, when repair parts are not a responsible fix, or when the household’s use no longer matches the heater that is there. It is not reasonable because a webpage named an age. Tanks fail early and late. Ask the provider what failed, what the replacement fuel and volume would be, and what has to change around the unit: platform, vent, gas line, electrical circuit, pan, or drain.

Permits are part of the Long Beach conversation. The [permit center](https://longbeach.gov/lbcd/building/permit-center/) is the public counter, at [(562) 570-5237](tel:+15625705237). Confirm whether the scope in front of you needs a permit and an inspection. A like-for-like tank in a closet is not the same paperwork as a move to tankless with a new vent through a wall. Do not treat a contractor’s verbal “no permit” as the city’s answer.

## Water Heaters - Tankless {#water-heaters-tankless}

Tankless heaters heat water as it passes through, so they do not store a tank of hot water. They trade tank space for a requirement the building has to meet: enough fuel or electrical capacity, a proper vent or condensate path, and maintenance so the heat exchanger does not scale up. The photograph on this page shows one way exterior units are caged and piped. It is somebody else’s installation. It is not a specification.

Choosing tankless because a neighbor likes it skips the sizing conversation. Count simultaneous showers and appliances. Say whether the gas meter and the flue path can change. Hard water is a maintenance question for tankless equipment, not a ban and not a requirement. The provider has to say what descaling would involve and how often they recommend it for the unit they would install.

## Tankless Water Heater - Install {#tankless-water-heater-install}

An install quote should name the model family only if the provider is actually proposing it, plus the fuel, the vent type, the condensate drain, the gas or electrical upgrade if one is required, and the permit path. Exterior placement, clearances, and noise matter in small lots and in condo enclosures. [Seal Beach](/areas/seal-beach/) and coastal parts of Long Beach raise an equipment-location question. They do not prove that salt air will destroy a unit. Ask about placement and shelter. Do not accept a corrosion forecast as a fact about your wall.

This preview does not confirm that tankless installation is accepted on a referred call in every ZIP. Treat the section as a checklist for a quote, not as a product offer.

## Tankless Water Heater - Repair {#tankless-water-heater-repair}

Tankless repairs are diagnostic. Error codes, scale, a failed sensor, a vent problem, or an undersized gas supply can look alike from the fixture. A technician has to be at the unit. Do not cycle power repeatedly as a troubleshooting script from this site. If you smell gas, leave. If the unit is leaking onto equipment below it, shut the water off only if you can reach the valve safely, and then ask for a visit.

Parts availability and brand-specific service are the provider’s constraints. This referral service is not a manufacturer’s service desk. If the quote is a repair, ask what was diagnosed. If the quote jumps to replacement, ask what cannot be repaired and what the new install would have to change.

:::faq
Q: Should a water heater be repaired or replaced?
A: Repair when the failure is a part the provider can stand behind and the vessel is sound. Replace when the tank leaks, the heat exchanger is done, or the surrounding vent or fuel cannot safely support another repair. Age alone is not the rule this site will publish. Ask what failed and what the new unit would require around it.

Q: How do I choose between a tank and a tankless unit?
A: Match the choice to fuel, space, venting, simultaneous use, and maintenance, not to a generic efficiency claim. A tank stores water and needs room and a pan. A tankless unit needs enough fuel or power and a plan for scale. The provider should say which constraints your address actually has. This page will not promise a bill reduction.

Q: Why do heater quotes look so different?
A: Because the scope is different. One quote may be a like-for-like tank. Another may include a new vent, a gas upgrade, a platform, a pan, permits, and haul-away. Ask each quote to list those items. A lower number that omits the vent is not the same job. No typical Long Beach price is stated here.

Q: What maintenance is worth asking about?
A: For a tank, ask whether flushing and an anode check apply to that model. For tankless equipment, ask how descaling is done and who supplies the valves. Hard water makes the question reasonable. It does not set an interval for your house. Confirm that a referred provider even offers maintenance before you plan on it.

Q: What should the written quote include?
A: Fuel, capacity or input, location, vent and condensate changes, any electrical or gas work, permit and inspection, haul-away, and what is excluded. If seismic strapping or a drain pan is part of the job, it should be named. You contract with the provider for that list. This website does not issue the quote.
:::
