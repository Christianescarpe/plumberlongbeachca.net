## Commercial Plumbing {#commercial-plumbing}

:::conditional
Property types, after-hours access, and which commercial calls a provider will accept are unverified. Nothing on this page is a statement that restaurants, shops, or warehouses are currently covered.
:::

Call only if you can approve the work or you have the owner or manager who can. A front-desk employee describing a backup is useful. That person may still not be allowed to sign for a repair. Say your role. In a multi-tenant building the fixture may sit on a line shared with another suite. The neighbor’s approval, or the landlord’s, can be required before a cleanout is opened. [Downtown and Alamitos Beach](/areas/downtown-alamitos-beach/) and parts of [west-central Long Beach](/areas/wrigley-west-central/) have that pattern. It is a question, not a map of every building.

Describe the property in ordinary words: retail floor, office restroom, food counter, laundry room, warehouse restroom, or something else. “Commercial” alone does not tell a provider whether the job is a flushometer toilet, a floor drain, or a shared stack. If the work is a clogged restroom, the [drain guide](/services/drain-cleaning/#floor-laundry-drains) still applies. If several fixtures back up, the [sewer guide](/services/sewer-repair/#sewer-lines) applies, including the city’s lateral split for Long Beach addresses. Do not paste that split onto a business in another city.

## What to have ready, and what is excluded until confirmed {#what-to-prepare}

Have the street address, the suite, the hours the plumber could enter, and any alarm or lock rule. Have a note of which fixtures failed and whether a floor drain is involved. If a contractor was on site recently, say what they did. You do not need to diagnose the pipe.

Grease traps, interceptors, and kitchen equipment under a health permit are not offered on the strength of this page. Long Beach Utilities warns that fats, oils, and grease can obstruct the public main and that a property owner can be held responsible for that damage. That is a reason to keep grease out of drains. It is not a promise that a referred plumber pumps traps or is accepted by the health department. Ask the provider directly, and expect “not our scope” to be a complete answer.

Industrial piping, chemical lines, and large process systems are outside the ordinary commercial description used here. [Carson](/areas/carson/) has substantial business and industrial property. A Carson address still needs a residential-versus-business sort, and industrial capability is not assumed. Institutions and unusual ZIP codes inside Long Beach, such as postal boxes or campus-style codes, are not treated as homeowner neighborhoods anywhere on this site. Classify them before anyone talks about a visit.

Hours are part of the scope. A restroom that can be closed on a weekday morning is a different job from a kitchen that cannot stop service. This preview has no confirmed answering hours and no after-hours plan. Do not read the utility’s all-hours emergency line as if it were a contractor’s promise to arrive. For sewage in the street or a manhole problem in Long Beach, call [(562) 570-2390](tel:+15625702390). For a gas odor, leave and use the gas emergency path on the [gas page](/services/gas-line-plumbing/#gas-lines-installed-repaired).

## Shared systems and repeated backups {#shared-systems}

A backup that returns in a multi-tenant building needs a camera conversation more often than another quick cable. Ask whether a cleanout serves one suite or the building. Ask who pays if the defect is in a shared lateral. The [camera section](/services/sewer-repair/#in-line-video-camera-service) explains what a recording should include. It does not guarantee that a commercial camera call is accepted.

Floor drains in a back room or a mechanical closet collect a different mix than a lavatory. Lint, grit, and occasional discharge from equipment are reasons to describe the room. They are not a diagnosis. If the only symptom is a single toilet in a customer restroom, say that. Overstating the job as “the whole building” sends the wrong preparation.

Quotes for commercial work should name the suite, the authorized signer, the fixtures, the hours, and any exclusion such as grease equipment, roof work, or electrical. Price follows access and scope. This site will not publish a commercial rate card.

:::faq
Q: Who is allowed to request a commercial plumbing visit?
A: The owner, or a manager with authority to approve the work and the entry. An employee can describe the symptom. The provider still needs a person who can authorize the suite and, if the line is shared, the landlord or the other tenant. Say your role at the start. This website cannot take that approval through a form, because it does not use forms.

Q: What should I prepare before asking about availability?
A: The address and suite, your authority, the fixtures that failed, whether a floor drain is involved, and the hours someone can enter. Mention alarms, gates, and whether the business can pause in that area. You do not need pipe material or a diagnosis. If grease equipment is part of the request, ask outright whether it is in scope. Assume it is not until the provider says it is.

Q: How do multi-tenant buildings change the job?
A: A stack or lateral may serve more than one suite, and the person with the backup may not control the cleanout. The quote should say whose pipe is affected and who must approve opening it. Repeated backups are a reason to ask about a camera, not to clear the same fixture again. On a Long Beach address, the utility’s lateral policy still applies.

Q: Will someone come after our closing time?
A: Only if the provider agrees to that hour. Many limit nights, weekends, or a kitchen that cannot shut down. Ask for the limit before you count on an after-close visit. For sewage in the street or a gas odor, use the utility or 911 rather than waiting on a contractor’s schedule.

Q: What must the quote identify?
A: The suite, the signer, the fixtures, the access window, and the exclusions. If a camera, a permit, or a return visit is possible, it should be a separate line from the first clearing. Grease traps, roof drains, and industrial piping should be marked in or out. You would contract with the provider for that list. This referral service does not negotiate the contract or collect the repair payment.
:::
