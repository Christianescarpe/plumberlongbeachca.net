Toilets, faucets, sinks, tubs, showers, and disposals come down to the supply, the drain, and the fixture. Tile, cabinets, and wiring stay out of the plumbing quote unless the quote names them. Long Beach houses and condos mix older valves with later remodels, so describe the opening you have and the one you want. A provider still has to see both.

## General Plumbing Repair {#general-plumbing-repair}

General repair is the catch-all for a dripping shutoff, a running toilet, a loose supply line, or a trap that weeps. It is also where people land when they are not sure which guide fits. If the symptom is a clog, start with [drain cleaning](/services/drain-cleaning/#drain-cleaning-unclogging). If several fixtures are weak, start with [pressure](/services/pipe-water-line-repair/#water-pressure-problems). If the ceiling is wet and no fixture was used, start with [leak detection](/services/leak-detection/#leaks-detected-repaired). This section is for the repair that stays at a fixture.

Say how long it has dripped, whether a shutoff holds, and whether a previous repair is visible. Do not describe it as an emergency unless water is uncontrolled or there is a safety issue in the safety note. This page does not set an arrival time.

## Sinks, Toilets & Faucets {#sinks-toilets-faucets}

A toilet that runs can be a fill valve, a flapper, or a leak at the base. Those are different repairs, and a base leak that has reached the floor is also a subfloor question the plumbing quote may not include. A faucet that drips from the spout is often the cartridge or washer. A faucet that drips from the supply line is the connection, not the cartridge. A sink that drains slowly is a [drain](/services/drain-cleaning/#one-fixture-or-several) question unless the basin itself is cracked.

Kitchen and bath faucets are not interchangeable. Holes in the sink, the spout reach, and the shutoffs under the cabinet decide what will fit. If you already bought a faucet, say so. Customer-supplied fixtures are accepted only when the provider agrees they match the openings and will stand behind the installation. A fixture that does not fit is not a defect in the house until someone checks the dimensions.

## Faucets, Fixtures and Pipes - Repair or Replace {#faucets-fixtures-and-pipes}

Repair keeps the fixture and replaces the failed part. Replacement changes the fixture. The pipe behind it is a third decision. A corroded shutoff under a [California Heights](/areas/bixby-knolls-california-heights/) lavatory may need to be cut out even when the faucet is fine. That short pipe repair belongs in the quote. A whole-house repipe does not. Send building-wide piping to the [pipe guide](/services/pipe-water-line-repair/#pipe-replacements-repairs).

Mineral crust on a chrome spout is common where water is moderately hard to hard. Crust is not a leak and not a safety failure. If you want it gone, ask whether the provider will descale or replace the finished part. Do not read hardness as a notice that the water is unsafe. The utility’s own wording is a mineral description.

## Fixtures Installed {#fixtures-installed}

Installation means setting a new fixture on an existing opening, or on an opening a remodel has already framed. The plumbing scope is the supply connections, the drain, and the fixture seals. It is not painting, and it is not a new vanity unless the quote says the vanity is included. Ask who disconnects the old fixture and who hauls it away.

New installations in a finished room still need the water off and, in a condo, sometimes a shutoff that affects a neighbor. [Downtown and Alamitos Beach](/areas/downtown-alamitos-beach/) buildings often have that constraint. Bring the building rules to the call. This site does not have them.

## Bathtub Install or Replace {#bathtub-install-or-replace}

A tub replacement can be a plumbing job and a surrounding-finish job at the same time. The drain, the overflow, and the valve are plumbing. The tile or the surround that has to come off to reach them may be someone else’s trade. If the quote does not name the surround, assume it is excluded and ask. A drop-in tub and a built-in tub do not leave the same opening.

Access in a small [Wrigley](/areas/wrigley-west-central/) cottage or a historic bungalow can matter more than the tub model. Door width, stair turns, and a cast-iron tub that has to leave in pieces change the labor. Measure before you purchase a tub the provider has not agreed to set. Waste and overflow parts should match the tub you actually have.

## Shower Install or Replace {#shower-install-or-replace}

A shower install replaces or adds the valve, the head, the arm, and sometimes the drain. A valve in the wall is a different opening from a trim-only swap. If the wall is tiled, cutting tile is not automatically included. Say whether the shower is a tub-shower combination or a separate stall.

New showers in an addition need supply, a vented drain, and a permit question. That is the remodel section below, not a trim kit. Anti-scald valves are a code conversation for the contractor and the inspector. This page will not tell you that your existing valve is illegal. It will tell you to ask.

## Showers & Tubs {#showers-tubs}

Repairing a shower or tub means the drip, the slow drain, the loose spout, or the diverter that will not send water to the head. Replacing means a new fixture. Keep those words apart on the call. A slow tub is often a [drain cleaning](/services/drain-cleaning/#floor-laundry-drains) visit. A tub that rocks, or a pan that leaks into the ceiling below, is a fixture and possibly a subfloor visit.

Shared bathrooms in a rental need the owner’s approval before finish materials are removed. A tenant who authorizes tile demolition can create a dispute the provider cannot solve. Say who is allowed to approve the opening.

## Garbage Disposals {#garbage-disposals}

A disposal is a fixture on the sink drain. Repair may be a jam or a failed unit. Replacement is a new unit on the existing opening, if the opening and the electrical connection cooperate. The electrical connection is not a plumbing inclusion unless the quote says an electrician is part of the job, or the plumber is qualified and the quote covers it. Do not assume either.

Dishwashers and disposals share a drain arm in many kitchens. Say if the dishwasher is backing up too. That can be the disposal, the arm, or a clog farther down the [kitchen line](/services/drain-cleaning/#drain-cleaning-unclogging). Reset buttons and hex keys are manufacturer steps, not a procedure this site will teach. If the unit hums and does not turn, stop using it and ask for a visit rather than forcing it.

## Plumbing for a Remodel or Addition - Install {#plumbing-for-remodel-or-addition}

:::conditional
Remodel plumbing is discussed here so the scope is clear. Permit requirements and acceptance of addition work are not confirmed for every address.
:::

An addition or a moved bathroom needs supply, drain, and vent planned before the fixture is chosen. The [Long Beach permit center](https://longbeach.gov/lbcd/building/permit-center/) is where building-permit questions go, at [(562) 570-5237](tel:+15625705237). A new sewer connection, if the addition needs one, follows the utility’s separate [sewer-connection process](https://www.lbutilities.org/Development-Services/Sewer-Development-Services/install-sewer-connection), which is not a same-week assumption.

Historic districts add a planning question when the work is visible or when the district’s ordinance reaches the scope. Belmont Heights, Bluff Heights, Bluff Park, Rose Park, Willmore and Drake Park, California Heights, the Grant block, and the Wrigley cottage streets are designated areas with published boundaries. Interior fixture swaps are not automatically reviewed, and they are not automatically free of review. Call historic preservation at [(562) 570-6194](tel:+15625706194) about the specific project. Do not rely on a neighbor’s remodel as precedent.

Customer-supplied fixtures, tile, and cabinets need a written line that says who buys them and who installs them. If the plumbing quote stops at the valve and the drain, the other trades need their own agreements. This referral service does not hire those trades and does not negotiate their contracts.

Ask the provider to include stuck shutoffs in the look. Stuck shutoffs are a pipe detail, not a sign the neighborhood’s plumbing has failed.

:::faq
Q: Is a leak at a faucet a reason to replace the fixture?
A: Often it is a cartridge, a washer, or a supply connection, and the fixture can stay. Replace it when the body is cracked, parts are unavailable, or you are changing the layout anyway. Ask the provider which of those is true. A spotty finish from hard water is a cleaning or finish question, not proof the valve failed.

Q: Can I supply the faucet, toilet, or tub myself?
A: Only if the provider agrees in the quote. The fixture has to match the holes, the drain, and the shutoffs, and someone has to own the warranty if the fixture itself is defective. A customer-supplied tub that does not fit through the door is not the provider’s mistake unless they measured it and approved it. Put that agreement in writing.

Q: What is included in remodel plumbing, and what is not?
A: Include supply, drain, vent, and the fixture connections the quote names. Do not assume tile, glass, cabinets, painting, or electrical work. Additions also raise permit and, sometimes, sewer-connection questions with the city. If a sentence is missing from the quote, it is not included. This website will not fill that gap later.

Q: Do historic districts stop ordinary fixture repairs?
A: Not as a blanket rule. Long Beach publishes district boundaries and a preservation phone line so you can ask about the scope you actually have. An interior valve replacement is a different question from a street-facing change. Call preservation and the permit center rather than guessing from the age of the block. The provider should do the same before promising a start date.

Q: What should a fixture quote list?
A: The fixture or the part, the connections, whether shutoffs are included, who opens tile or a cabinet, haul-away, and any permit. For a tub or shower, say whether the surround stays. For a disposal, say whether electrical work is included. Price follows that list. No citywide fixture price is published here.
:::
