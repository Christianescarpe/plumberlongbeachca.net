Start with the symptom. A clog, a leak, no hot water, a fixture, or a whole building each has its own guide and the questions that change a quote. The [homepage](/) is the page for general plumbing help in Long Beach.

A bathroom sink that will not drain is a different visit from sewage at a cleanout, a water heater with no flame, or a manager calling about a shared line. If you smell gas, or sewage is in the street, or a water main is broken, use the utility numbers in the safety note.

## The nine clusters {#nine-clusters}

[Drain cleaning](/services/drain-cleaning/) covers a stopped fixture, a floor or laundry drain, cable cleaning, and hydro-jetting when a provider judges the pipe suitable. Repeated backups that may be in the lateral belong with [sewer line repair](/services/sewer-repair/), including camera inspection and the City's split between the owner's lateral section, the shared section, and the main.

[Leak detection](/services/leak-detection/) is about finding a likely zone before anyone talks about opening a wall or a slab. It does not guarantee a method. [Pipe and water line repair](/services/pipe-water-line-repair/) separates a private supply line from the municipal main, and pressure at one faucet from pressure everywhere. [Water heaters](/services/water-heaters/) covers storage tanks and tankless equipment, including the fact that Long Beach Utilities delivers unheated water.

[Fixtures and remodel plumbing](/services/fixtures-remodel-plumbing/) is toilets, sinks, faucets, tubs, showers, and disposals, plus the plumbing portion of a remodel. It does not include tile, cabinets, or electrical work by default. [Gas line plumbing](/services/gas-line-plumbing/) is for planned appliance piping. A suspected leak is an evacuation and a utility call.

[Commercial plumbing](/services/commercial-plumbing/) asks who may authorize the visit and what the property type is. Grease traps and industrial systems are not assumed. [Specialty plumbing](/services/specialty-plumbing/) holds treatment equipment, backflow, sump pumps, manufactured homes, pool plumbing, hydronic radiant heat, and wells. Several of those are conditional until a provider confirms them.

## How to use a guide {#how-to-use-a-guide}

Each service page names the decisions that change a quote: access, who owns the affected pipe, whether a permit or a camera is likely, and what this site will not claim. Local notes are tied to published Long Beach sources. They are not stories from a crew, because this referral service does not have field crews to quote.

If you already know the neighborhood, the [area guides](/areas/) add housing and access context and link back to the service sections that matter there. Read [how referrals work](/how-referrals-work/) before you treat any sentence on these pages as an offer to perform construction.
