When several drains fail together, or the same clog comes back, the question has usually moved from one fixture to the sewer line. In Long Beach, Long Beach Utilities describes two sections of the lateral plus the city main. The pipe under a yard is not automatically the city’s, and it is not automatically yours all the way to the street. This page explains that split. A provider still has to find the cleanout and recommend the repair.

## Sewer Lines {#sewer-lines}

The utility’s published account is specific. The section from the building to the sidewalk or property line, commonly about four inches and on private property, is the owner’s responsibility. The next section, from that property line to the center of the street or alley, commonly about six inches and on city property, is a shared responsibility. Routine maintenance of both sections is the property owner’s job under the policy the utility cites. The city maintains the main, and it describes its own work on laterals that lie on city property.

Those diameters are the utility’s word “commonly.” They are not a measurement of a house in [Belmont Shore](/areas/belmont-shore-heights/) or [North Long Beach](/areas/north-long-beach/). A claim against the city, a backup into a finished floor, and a repair in the parkway are all fact-specific. This page is not a claim form and not a legal opinion.

If sewage is in the street, a manhole is noisy or smelling, or insects are coming from a manhole, the utility tells you to call [(562) 570-2390](tel:+15625702390). The same number is what it lists for a sewer stoppage on the property. Calling the utility does not hire a contractor. It does put the public system on notice when the public system is the right party.

An in-line camera is a recording from inside the pipe. It is useful when a [drain cleaning](/services/drain-cleaning/#hydro-jetting-service) opened the line and the backup returned, when several fixtures fail together, or when someone wants you to approve a repair you cannot see. A useful delivery is the recording, the distance to defects, and a plain statement of breaks, offsets, roots, or grease. A sales narration without the file is not the same thing.

Camera work is not a plumbing inspection of the whole house. Supply leaks, water heaters, and fixture valves are outside that recording. The [leak guide](/services/leak-detection/#plumbing-inspection) is the page for that broader look. A camera also cannot be promised where there is no access point. The provider has to say where the camera will enter.

## Repair, replacement, and methods that are not promised {#repair-versus-replacement}

Cleaning removes a blockage. Repair addresses a defect at a point. Replacement addresses a run that cannot be relied on. Which of those is true depends on the recording, the depth, the surface above the pipe, and whether the defect is on the owner’s section or the shared section. A website cannot pick among them from a ZIP code.

Trenchless methods exist in the trade. They are not accepted here as a default, and they are not described as approved by the City for your address. Some pipes are collapsed, some yards have no room to set equipment, and some defects still need an open excavation. If a quote uses the word trenchless, it should name the method, the length, the access pits, and what happens if the pipe will not accept the liner or the burst. Do not treat a brochure word as a scope.

New sewer connections, and some repairs that excavate in the street, follow a permit path. Long Beach Utilities describes a sewer-connection review, drawings, capacity questions, fees, and an excavation permit through Public Works. The published processing talk is measured in weeks, not in a same-day slogan. Read the [connection page](https://www.lbutilities.org/Development-Services/Sewer-Development-Services/install-sewer-connection) as the utility’s process, then let the contractor and the city apply it to the actual work. Call 811 before digging. That notice is damage prevention, not a plumbing tutorial.

## What an estimate has to identify {#what-an-estimate-identifies}

Ask the quote to separate investigation from repair. A camera, a locate, and a permit fee are not the same line as pipe replacement. Ask which section of the lateral is affected, who is expected to maintain it, and whether the city has to be involved because the work is in the right of way. Ask what surface will be opened and who restores it.

Shared buildings need one more line: who signed the authorization. A backup in a fourplex near [downtown](/areas/downtown-alamitos-beach/) may involve a lateral that more than one unit uses. The resident with wet flooring is not always the person who can approve excavation. None of this is a price. Access, depth, restoration, and jurisdiction are why two sewer quotes for “the same backup” diverge.

:::faq
Q: Who is responsible for the sewer line at a Long Beach house?
A: The utility says the building-to-property-line section is the owner’s, and the property-line-to-main section is shared, with routine maintenance of both sections on the owner. The city maintains the main and describes repair duties on the city side. Common diameters are typical, not a survey. A specific backup can still need both a contractor and the utility.

Q: Is a camera the same thing as a cleaning?
A: No. Cleaning tries to open a stoppage. A camera records the pipe so someone can see breaks, offsets, or roots. You can need one, the other, or both. Ask for the recording and the distances, and keep that file separate from any later repair proposal. This site does not perform the inspection.

Q: Should I approve replacement the first time a drain backs up?
A: Not from the backup alone. A soft clog, grease, or a single root intrusion can look dramatic and still be a cleaning. Replacement is a response to pipe condition, location, and how much of the run is failing. Ask what the camera showed and which lateral section is involved before you compare dig and no-dig options.

Q: Sewage is in the street. Is that the same call as a tub that will not drain?
A: Street sewage, an overflowing manhole, or a neighborhood odor from a manhole belongs with Long Beach Utilities at its published emergency number. A tub that will not drain, with the street dry, may still be a private branch or lateral. The utility also lists property stoppages on that emergency line, so telling them is appropriate. A contractor is an additional question, not a replacement for that call.

Q: What should a sewer estimate spell out?
A: It should separate the investigation from the repair, name the footage and the access point, and say whether the work is on the house side of the property line or toward the main. It should list permits, surface restoration, and who is authorized to approve it. Method words such as trenchless need the same detail. No citywide price belongs on this page.
:::
