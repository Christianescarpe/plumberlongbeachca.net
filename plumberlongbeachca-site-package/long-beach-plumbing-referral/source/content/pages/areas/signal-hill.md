Plumbing help for a Signal Hill address starts by naming the city. Signal Hill is its own municipality, not a historic district of Long Beach, and it does not inherit Long Beach Utilities’ sewer diagram. The 2025 Census internal point sits about 1.6 miles from Long Beach’s internal point. That figure is a straight-line planning distance, checked on November 8, 2026. It is not a drive time and not proof that a provider accepts the address.

The approved map for this site associates Signal Hill with ZIP 90755. Treat that as a label to confirm, not as a boundary. Streets along the city limit can look like Long Beach until the jurisdiction line says otherwise. Give the full address. If the water bill, the trash bill, or the city hall on the permit is Signal Hill’s, do not use a Long Beach permit number or a Long Beach lateral policy to explain the job.

The city’s own website is [cityofsignalhill.org](https://cityofsignalhill.org/). This guide does not restate Signal Hill’s municipal code. Building, encroachment, and utility questions belong to that city and to whatever water, sewer, and gas providers serve the specific parcel. Some Long Beach pages name Long Beach Utilities because that city operates those systems. Do not copy the phone numbers onto a Signal Hill emergency as if they were automatic. Ask which agency covers the address. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

## What a call should establish {#what-a-call-should-establish}

Start with authority and access, the same way a [downtown Long Beach](/areas/downtown-alamitos-beach/) building would, but do not assume the building rules match. Signal Hill has houses, hillside lots, and multi-unit buildings. Who may approve a repair in a multi-unit building is a lease or an ownership question. This website does not keep owner lists. A tenant should say so. A manager should say which units share a shutoff.

The plumbing topics that most often belong here are a [leak](/services/leak-detection/#leaks-detected-repaired), a [private water line](/services/pipe-water-line-repair/#water-lines-installed-repaired), and a [fixture](/services/fixtures-remodel-plumbing/#general-plumbing-repair). A sewer backup still needs a diagnosis. The Long Beach split between the owner’s lateral section and the shared section is a Long Beach Utilities policy. Signal Hill’s arrangement may differ. Until you have Signal Hill’s own description, the honest sentence is “the provider and the city have to say whose pipe it is.”

Do not assert that slope, oil history, or soil movement causes leaks on the hill. Those would be site investigations, not page content. California Heights, over the line in Long Beach, even notes that some bungalows were moved from Signal Hill in the 1920s. That is a Long Beach preservation fact. It is not a pipe inventory of either city.
