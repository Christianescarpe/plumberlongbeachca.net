## Private water lines {#private-water-lines}

A private water line is the piping on the customer’s side of the meter, once you know where the local supplier says the meter responsibility ends. That sentence is deliberately generic. Long Beach Utilities’ “to the meter” wording is about Long Beach. Carson’s supplier may draw the line differently. Ask. The [pipe guide](/services/pipe-water-line-repair/#water-main) explains why a public main is not a job this referral sells, and that caution travels even when the agency changes.

Water in the street, a broken public main, or a hydrant failure belongs to the utility that owns it. A leak under a slab or a spinning meter with the house shut off may be private. The [leak guide](/services/leak-detection/#when-the-utility-should-hear) is the method. It is not a map of Carson’s mains. Excavation means 811 and the city’s permit, not a Long Beach Public Works counter.

Quotes should name who is authorized, whether the address is a house or a business, and what is excluded. Timing and price are the provider’s. This page will not estimate either.
