Bixby Knolls and California Heights sit in the 90807 conversation, north of the downtown grid and well inland from Belmont Shore. They are neighbors. They are not the same historic district. This page uses the City’s record for California Heights and treats Bixby Knolls as the surrounding area callers name, without pretending the ordinance covers every Knolls house.

California Heights is the City’s largest historic district, nearly 1,500 homes, built out of the 1920s boom. Spanish Colonial Revival houses predominate, with Tudor, Norman, neo-traditional, and some California bungalows moved in from other neighborhoods, including houses relocated from Signal Hill. The City credits the streetscape itself: vintage streetlights and large, mature trees. The boundary streets it publishes are Wardlow Road, Bixby Road, Lime Avenue, and Gardenia Avenue. The expansion ordinance date it lists is August 29, 2000.

Bixby Knolls, along the Atlantic and Long Beach Boulevard side of this part of the city, includes commercial corners and houses that are outside that rectangle. If your address is not inside the Wardlow–Bixby–Lime–Gardenia box, do not describe it as the historic district in a permit call. You can still use this guide for the ZIP section and the service links. Wardlow Road is also where people orient themselves. It is not, by itself, a plumbing condition.

## Age is not a pipe report {#age-is-not-a-pipe-report}

A 1920s house can still have original fragments, a 1970s repipe, or a kitchen redone last year. The district survey counted architectural style. It did not televise sewers. Anyone who tells you California Heights “is all clay” or “is all galvanized” is ahead of the evidence this site will repeat. The honest step is a look: visible piping under a sink for a fixture job, or a [camera](/services/sewer-repair/#in-line-video-camera-service) when backups repeat.

Repair versus replacement is the same discipline as everywhere else in Long Beach. A failed fitting is not a whole-house [repipe](/services/pipe-water-line-repair/#pipe-replacements-repairs). A line with several defects might be. The provider has to show which. Mature street trees are a reason a sewer camera sometimes finds roots. They are not a finding for your parkway until the recording exists. The City’s tree policies are a separate public-works topic if a claim involves a street tree. Do not start there.

Pressure that is weak at one lavatory and fine at the hose bib is a [fixture or branch](/services/pipe-water-line-repair/#water-pressure-problems) question. Pressure that is weak on the whole block is a utility question at [(562) 570-2390](tel:+15625702390) before it is a private repipe. 90807 does not change the meter boundary. Long Beach Utilities still describes its water responsibility as ending at the meter.

## Remodels and access on these lots {#remodels-and-access}

Spanish Colonial houses often hide piping in plaster walls that owners do not want opened casually. Say that when you describe a [shower valve](/services/fixtures-remodel-plumbing/#shower-install-or-replace) or a repipe. A quote that ignores plaster repair is not the same quote as one that includes it, and plaster may be a different trade. Historic status matters when the work changes the exterior or otherwise falls under the ordinance. Interior pipe is a question for preservation at [(562) 570-6194](tel:+15625706194), not a guess from the tile roof in the 1927 photograph on this page.

Bixby Knolls storefronts and upstairs apartments are [commercial or mixed-use](/services/commercial-plumbing/#commercial-plumbing) intakes. The person who can approve a restroom repair may be a tenant, a landlord, or both. Bring that to the call. This guide does not rate restaurants or promise grease-trap service.

:::faq
Q: Does the age of a California Heights house establish the pipe?
A: No. The City’s district record establishes style, era, and a streetscape, including houses from the 1920s boom and nearly 1,500 homes inside Wardlow, Bixby, Lime, and Gardenia. It does not record galvanized, copper, plastic, or clay at any address. Additions and repipes since then are invisible from the sidewalk. Ask for the material you can see, or a camera where the symptom is in the sewer.

Q: When is an inspection more useful than another repair?
A: When the same backup returns, when more than one fixture is involved, or when a quote jumps to replacement without showing the pipe. A camera file with distances is the inspection that matches a sewer decision. A fixture drip does not need that file. It needs someone to say whether the cartridge, the shutoff, or a short pipe is the failure. House age does not choose between those visits.

Q: What should we plan before a remodel opens a wall?
A: Know whether the address is inside the historic district, and ask preservation and the permit center about the actual scope before an exterior vent or equipment change is scheduled. Inside, decide who patches plaster and whether shutoffs work. A provider should list plumbing separately from patching. Bixby Knolls addresses outside the district still need ordinary permits. They do not need a historic label they have not earned.
:::
