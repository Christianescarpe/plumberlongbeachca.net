Ask by phone. Have the address and a short description of the drain, leak, heater, or fixture. There is no form and no upload. Utility lines lower on this page are for gas and for water or sewer emergencies. They are the city’s numbers.

:::if-production
Call {{phone}} during {{hours}}, Pacific Time. The person who answers should identify the call as a referral before connecting you. The provider still decides whether the job is accepted.
:::

## What to have in front of you {#what-to-have}

Write these down before you call. None of them is a form field here.

- The street address, unit, and ZIP, and whether the city is Long Beach or a neighbor such as Signal Hill.
- Whether you own, rent, or manage, and who else must approve entry or a shared shutoff.
- What is happening: one fixture or several, hot or cold, how long, and whether water is reaching the street.
- Access limits: gates, associations, parking, pets, and whether the property is occupied.
- For a heater or a fixture replacement, the fuel and whether you already bought the equipment.

Do not send photos to this website. There is no upload. If a provider later wants a picture, they will say how. Do not email a lead. No address is published for that purpose.

## Hours and emergencies {#hours-and-emergencies}

Long Beach Utilities says its water and sewer emergency line answers at all hours. That line is the utility, at [(562) 570-2390](tel:+15625702390). The gas emergency line is [(562) 570-2140](tel:+15625702140). Both were rechecked on the utility’s site on November 8, 2026. Reconfirm them if you are reading this later.

If you are outside Long Beach, those utility numbers may be the wrong agency. [Signal Hill](/areas/signal-hill/), [Lakewood](/areas/lakewood/), [Seal Beach](/areas/seal-beach/), [Los Alamitos](/areas/los-alamitos/), and [Carson](/areas/carson/) each say so on their own guides. Use 911 when you are unsure and someone could be hurt.

:::faq
Q: Can I request a quote without calling?
A: No. This service does not take forms, chat leads, or email jobs. A quote has to come from the provider after a conversation about the address and the scope. The preview cannot start that conversation. Utility emergency numbers are for the hazards they name, not for a remodel estimate.

Q: What number do I use tonight if something is dangerous?
A: In Long Beach, gas odor means leave and call (562) 570-2140 or 911 from a safe place. Sewage in the street, a bad manhole, or a water main failure means (562) 570-2390. Do not wait on the referral control, which does not dial. Outside Long Beach, use 911 or the local utility you have confirmed. This site will not guess the agency.

Q: Will you text or email a confirmation?
A: Not from this website. There is no messaging program and no form to confirm. A live referral call, later, would be voice. Any future texting would need its own approval and a privacy update before it appeared. Assume it does not exist.
:::
