Where the guides actually go

Questions callers ask first

Are you the plumber who would do the work?

No. This site is an independent referral service, not a plumbing contractor and not a city department. If a call is connected, a separate provider decides whether to accept it, what the price is, and what the license and scope cover. You would contract with and pay that provider directly.

How do I ask whether someone can take the job?

Use the phone button on this page. There is no form. Have the address, who can approve the visit, and a short description of the drain, leak, or heater. The provider sets the price and the schedule. Utility emergency numbers in the safety note are for gas and for sewer or water emergencies.

Can you tell me what the job will cost?

No. A useful estimate depends on access, what the fixture or pipe actually is, whether a camera or permit is involved, and who may approve the work. Those facts belong in the provider's quote. This site does not publish a price list or a typical Long Beach total.

Do you already know my ZIP is accepted?

The guides name eleven Long Beach residential ZIP sections and five nearby cities so the site has a map you can read. That list is not a live coverage feed. Acceptance for houses, condos, rentals, and businesses has to be confirmed when a real line is answered.

What should I do before I describe a backup or a gas smell?

If you smell gas, leave the area and call Long Beach Utilities or 911 from a safe place. If sewage is in the street or a manhole is overflowing, call the utility water and sewer emergency line. For a private fixture, note who can approve entry before you ask a contractor to visit.

If there is danger, use the utility line

For a suspected gas leak in Long Beach, get people out of the area. From a safe place, call Long Beach Utilities at (562) 570-2140 or call 911. Do not use switches, flames, or a phone where you smell gas. This referral preview is not the gas emergency line.

For sewage in the street, a noisy or odorous manhole, or a sewer stoppage Long Beach Utilities asks to hear about, call (562) 570-2390. Those numbers are the utility’s published emergency lines, checked November 8, 2026. They are not this website’s referral number.
