Plumbing coverage areas

Pick a Long Beach neighborhood or a nearby city, then the plumbing problem. Six guides cover Long Beach. Five more cover separate cities. A ZIP code is a postal area. It is not a city limit, and it is not proof that a provider accepts the address today.

Distances, where they appear, are straight-line miles from the 2025 Census internal point for Long Beach city, latitude 33.780791 and longitude −118.168180, to the internal point of the other place. They were checked against the 2025 Census Gazetteer on November 8, 2026. They are not drive times, not a radius from city hall, and not a promise that every address in a city falls inside a 20-mile planning circle.

How to read this map

Is a ZIP code enough to know I am covered?

No. The eleven Long Beach sections and the five nearby cities are how this site is organized. Acceptance of houses, condos, rentals, and businesses is not verified, and a ZIP crosses neighborhood and sometimes city lines. When a phone line exists, the address is what has to be checked. Until then, the pages are reading material, not a yes.

Why isn’t there a separate page for every ZIP?

A cloned ZIP page would repeat the same plumbing advice under a new number. The districts already name the ZIP sections and add housing facts that are actually different. A future ZIP page would need its own search intent and facts that would still help if the ZIP label were removed. PO Box and institutional codes are not candidates for homeowner pages.

Are Signal Hill and the other cities part of Long Beach?

No. Each has its own guide because the municipal rules differ. Signal Hill is the closest and is still a separate city. Do not hand a Lakewood, Seal Beach, Los Alamitos, or Carson address a Long Beach sewer diagram as if it were local law. The diagram on the sewer page is labeled as Long Beach Utilities’ description.
