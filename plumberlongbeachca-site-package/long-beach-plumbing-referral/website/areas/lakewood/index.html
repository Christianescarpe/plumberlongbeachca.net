Does a water-heater replacement need a Lakewood permit?

Ask Lakewood’s building counter and ask the provider to name the permit in the quote. Long Beach’s permit center phone does not answer Lakewood questions. A like-for-like tank and a conversion to tankless are different scopes and may not be treated alike. This page will not waive a permit and will not quote a fee. If the provider says no permit is required, ask them what they are relying on.

How do I tell a drain clog from a sewer problem?

One slow fixture, with the others clear, is usually a branch or a trap and starts as a cleaning. Several fixtures, a backup at a lower drain, or a clog that returns after a cleaning is a reason to look farther down the line, often with a camera, before anyone talks about replacement. Who owns that line in Lakewood is not the Long Beach lateral policy. Ask the provider and the city. Sewage in a public street is an emergency for the local utility, not a web form.
