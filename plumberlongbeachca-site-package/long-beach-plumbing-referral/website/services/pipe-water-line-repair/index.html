Pipe and Water Line Repair in Long Beach

Weak pressure, a supply line that leaks, or piping that needs repair starts with one question: is it one fixture, or the whole building? Long Beach Utilities delivers water to the meter. Piping after the meter is private. The age of the neighborhood does not tell you which pipe failed.

Pipe Replacements & Repairs

A repair replaces a failed fitting or a short run the provider can stand behind. A repipe replaces a larger share of the piping because the material, the leak history, or the layout no longer supports patching. Those are different approvals. A single pinhole is not a whole-house repipe, and a house full of layered patches is not automatically solved by one more coupling.

Ask what material is there now, what would replace it, and which portions would stay. Ask whether the work is exposed in a crawlspace or buried. Ask who opens walls and who patches them. This referral service does not select copper, PEX, or any other material for you, and it does not claim that a 1920s house in California Heights still has the pipe it was built with. Remodels change that. Only a look at the house changes the answer.

Water Lines Installed & Repaired

A private water line is the piping that carries water after the meter to the building and between fixtures. Installing a new run, for an addition or a relocated meter, is not the same permit conversation as repairing a leak in an existing run. The fixture and remodel guide covers the fixture end. This page covers the line itself.

Tell the provider whether the property is occupied, whether the water can be off for part of a day, and whether a tenant or a manager has to approve the shutoff. In a multi-unit building the shutoff may affect neighbors. That is an authorization fact, not a technical detail to skip.

Underground Pipe Repairs

Buried supply piping adds a locate, a surface, and sometimes a permit. Yards, driveways, and parkways are not interchangeable. Call 811 before digging so buried utilities can be marked. That call is damage prevention. It does not by itself produce a plumbing quote.

If the buried pipe is a sewer lateral, stop and use the sewer guide. The responsibility split the utility publishes for sewer laterals does not describe a water service. Mixing the two in one sentence is how the wrong party gets called. A leak search may be the step that decides which pipe is wet.

Water Pressure Problems

Pressure at one faucet, with normal flow everywhere else, usually points at that faucet, its aerator, its shutoff, or the short pipe serving it. Pressure that is weak at every cold fixture points at something shared: a valve at the meter, a regulator, a constriction in the private line, or a supply problem on the utility side. Hot-only weakness can be the heater or the hot branch, which is the water heater page, not a main.

Do not assume a regulator is missing or failed because a neighbor complained. Ask the provider to say which test was done and at which fixture. If the whole block is affected, the utility should hear about it. A private repipe does not fix a city main.

Water Main - Install, Replace or Repair

The municipal main is not a service this site sells

Replacing the city water main is utility work. A referred contractor is not presented here as the party who installs or repairs Long Beach’s public main. The private service line on the building side of the meter is the scope that can be discussed, and only if a provider accepts it.

Use (562) 570-2390 for a broken public main, water coming up in the street, or a hydrant failure, because that is the utility’s published emergency list. Use a contractor conversation for the private line after the meter, once the utility side has been ruled out. The diagram on this page is the distinction. It is not a locate of your meter.

Frozen Pipes & Drains Thawed

Availability has to be confirmed

Long Beach is not sold here as a freeze city. Thawing frozen pipes or drains is a rare, conditional request. Availability is not confirmed. This page does not give a thawing method. Open flames and improvised heaters are a fire and carbon-monoxide risk and are not instructions.

If a cold snap ever leaves a pipe frozen, the safe step is to shut the water off if you can do that without forcing a fitting, skip DIY heat, and ask a provider whether they will take the call. A burst after a thaw is a leak, and the leak guide applies. Most Long Beach calls in ordinary weather are not this one.

Winterization

Availability has to be confirmed

Winterization here means preparing a vacant or lightly used property, not a cold-climate shutdown. Acceptance is conditional and is not a featured local service.

For a house that will sit empty, the useful questions are who can enter, whether the water should be off at the meter, how the water heater is set, and who gets a leak notice. Irrigation shutoff and a hose bib that drips are maintenance items a provider may or may not include. Do not expect a freeze-protection package written for a mountain cabin. If the absence is short, say so. The scope should match the vacancy, not a template from another climate.

Long Beach context for supply piping

The utility’s drinking-water page says customers receive water up to the meter and that the supply is moderately hard to hard. Hardness explains scale in heaters and on fixtures. It is not proof that a neighborhood’s buried lines have failed. The City’s 1920s record for California Heights, and the earlier record for Belmont Heights and Willmore, is a reason to ask what is in the walls. It is not a materials report.

Permit questions for private plumbing go through the Long Beach permit center. Building permits are listed at (562) 570-5237. Whether a particular repipe needs a permit is a question for that counter and for the contractor, not a waiver printed here. Homes in designated historic districts may have additional planning questions if the work changes an exterior. Interior piping is not automatically exempt or automatically restricted. Ask about the actual scope.

Pipe and pressure questions

The pressure dropped. Is the line failing?

Not from the symptom alone. If one faucet is weak and the others are normal, start at that faucet and its shutoff. If every cold fixture is weak, ask whether the regulator, the private line, or the utility supply is involved. A provider has to measure it. A block-wide drop belongs with the utility before anyone sells a repipe.

When is a repipe the honest recommendation?

When the provider can show that patches are no longer reliable: repeated leaks, a material that is failing in more than one place, or a layout that cannot be repaired at the wet spot. Age of the house is background, not proof. Ask which runs would be replaced, which would stay, and what openings are required. Compare that scope, not a slogan.

How is a buried water line assessed?

By locating the meter and the likely path, watching for surface clues, and using whatever test the provider is willing to describe. 811 marking comes before excavation. The quote should say what surface may be opened and who restores it. A sewer camera does not inspect a water line. Keep those scopes apart.

Where does the utility’s pipe end?

Long Beach Utilities says its responsibility for drinking water runs to the meter. The private piping is on the building side. The public main in the street is not a contractor service offered on this page. If water is surfacing in the street, call the utility emergency line. If the meter spins with the house shut off, say that too, because the leak may be private.

Do I need a permit to repair or replace pipe?

Sometimes, and the answer depends on the scope. The Long Beach permit center is the public counter for building permits. A contractor who pulls a permit should be able to say which one. Historic-district questions are separate and depend on whether the work is only interior. This site will not tell you that your address is exempt.
