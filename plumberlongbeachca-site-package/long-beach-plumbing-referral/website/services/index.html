Plumbing services

Start with the symptom. A clog, a leak, no hot water, a fixture, or a whole building each has its own guide and the questions that change a quote. The homepage is the page for general plumbing help in Long Beach.

A bathroom sink that will not drain is a different visit from sewage at a cleanout, a water heater with no flame, or a manager calling about a shared line. If you smell gas, or sewage is in the street, or a water main is broken, use the utility numbers in the safety note.

The nine clusters

Drain cleaning covers a stopped fixture, a floor or laundry drain, cable cleaning, and hydro-jetting when a provider judges the pipe suitable. Repeated backups that may be in the lateral belong with sewer line repair, including camera inspection and the City's split between the owner's lateral section, the shared section, and the main.

Leak detection is about finding a likely zone before anyone talks about opening a wall or a slab. It does not guarantee a method. Pipe and water line repair separates a private supply line from the municipal main, and pressure at one faucet from pressure everywhere. Water heaters covers storage tanks and tankless equipment, including the fact that Long Beach Utilities delivers unheated water.

Fixtures and remodel plumbing is toilets, sinks, faucets, tubs, showers, and disposals, plus the plumbing portion of a remodel. It does not include tile, cabinets, or electrical work by default. Gas line plumbing is for planned appliance piping. A suspected leak is an evacuation and a utility call.

Commercial plumbing asks who may authorize the visit and what the property type is. Grease traps and industrial systems are not assumed. Specialty plumbing holds treatment equipment, backflow, sump pumps, manufactured homes, pool plumbing, hydronic radiant heat, and wells. Several of those are conditional until a provider confirms them.
