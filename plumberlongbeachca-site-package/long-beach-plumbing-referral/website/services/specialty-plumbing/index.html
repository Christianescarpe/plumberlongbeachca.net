Water Purification & Filters

Long Beach Utilities calls the drinking water moderately hard to hard. Hardness is calcium and magnesium. It shows up as scale and spots. The utility does not describe that fact as unsafe drinking water, and this page will not add a scare. A filter or a softener is a preference and a maintenance commitment. Filters that are not cleaned can grow a problem of their own. The utility says to follow the manufacturer’s care instructions.

Salt softeners swap calcium and magnesium for sodium. The utility notes that added sodium can matter for people on dialysis or with high blood pressure, heart disease, or kidney disease, and it says to ask a doctor. That is the utility’s caution, not medical advice from this website. Some households soften only the hot side. Whether a referred provider installs that arrangement is unconfirmed.

Water Treatment & Purification System - Install or Replace

Availability has to be confirmed

Installation or replacement of treatment equipment is conditional. This preview does not confirm acceptance.

An install quote should name the equipment, where it sits, what it treats, the drain or electrical needs, and who maintains it. It should not claim the city water is contaminated. If you have a test from the utility or a lab, bring it. This site does not sample water and does not interpret a lab report. Replacement of an old softener is the same discipline: say what is there, what is failing, and what the new unit would change about the plumbing.

Water Treatment & Purification System - Repair or Service

Availability has to be confirmed

Repair and service of treatment systems are conditional. A maintenance plan is not a current offer.

Service means the tasks the manufacturer and the provider actually list: media, filters, brine tank, or a control valve. “Service” without a list is not comparable. If the unit is leaking onto a floor, treat it as a leak until someone sees it. Do not bypass a softener with an improvised hose as a fix from this page.

Backflow Preventers

Testing and repair are different offers

Installing or repairing a backflow preventer is not the same thing as a certified test. Many water agencies require a specific tester and a filing. This site does not claim that a referred provider is that tester, and it does not claim Long Beach’s current filing rule. Confirm both the work and the test before you book anything.

If a device is leaking, say whether it is indoors or in a vault, and whether the city or a water supplier has already sent a notice. Bring the notice. Do not assume a plumbing repair call includes the signed test. Ask the question in those words.

Sump Pumps Installed & Repaired

Availability has to be confirmed

Sump pumps are listed because they are a real plumbing topic. Buyer coverage in Long Beach is not confirmed. Many houses in this climate have no sump.

A sump is a pit and a pump that lifts drainage water. Installation needs a pit, a discharge that does not create a nuisance, and often an electrical circuit that may be outside a plumber’s quote. Repair is a failed float, a failed pump, or a discharge line that is blocked. If you do not have a pit, you do not have this job. Do not let a rainy week turn a patio drain into an assumed sump. Patio and floor drains are on the drain page.

Plumbing for Mobile Homes

Availability has to be confirmed

Manufactured and mobile homes are conditional. Park rules, ownership of the coach versus the space, and acceptance are all unconfirmed.

The plumbing under a manufactured home is often accessible and sometimes restricted by the park. The person who owns the coach may not control the utility pedestal or the sewer connection at the space. Say which you are. Supply lines, drains, and water heaters exist, and the same safety rules for gas still apply. This is not a promise that a provider will enter a particular park. Ask the park what credentials and notice it requires, and ask the provider whether that park is in scope.

Pool, Spa & Jacuzzi Service

Availability has to be confirmed

Only the plumbing portion of a pool, spa, or jacuzzi is even a candidate. Cleaning, chemicals, heaters that are not plumbing, and electrical work are not included by this sentence. Acceptance is unconfirmed.

Plumbing here means supply and drain lines, a leak in the plumbing the provider agrees to look at, or a connection to the building’s water or sewer that is actually a plumbing scope. It does not mean weekly service, plaster, or a pump motor unless the quote says so. A leak in a pool shell can be a different trade. Say whether the water loss is in the equipment pad or the vessel. Leak detection on the house is a separate guide. Do not merge them into one visit without asking.

Radiant Floor Systems

Radiant floors that use heated water are a hydronic plumbing topic. Electric mats are not. This site does not take electrical radiant work, and the repeated heating category in a separate trade list is not copied onto these pages. Hydronic work can involve a boiler or a water heater, pumps, and tubing in a floor. It can also involve trades and permits this referral may never be able to route. Treat the next two sections as conditional descriptions, not offers.

Radiant Floor Heating System - Install

Availability has to be confirmed

New hydronic radiant installs are conditional and uncommon as a Long Beach referral. Classification, permits, and acceptance have to be confirmed. No design is offered here.

An install is a design problem: heat source, tubing layout, controls, and what the floor is made of. It is not a fixture swap. Ask whether the provider even does this work before you describe a whole house. If the answer is no, that is a successful call.

Radiant Floor Heating System - Repair

Availability has to be confirmed

Hydronic repairs are conditional. A cold floor has many causes, including the heat source and the controls, which may not be plumbing.

Describe whether any zone is warm, whether the heat source has hot water, and whether the system has worked before. Do not open tubing. A repair quote should say whether the visit is diagnosis only. Tubing buried in a slab is not a casual patch.

Well Pump - Install or Replace

Availability has to be confirmed

Well pumps are not assumed to be billable, or even present, on urban Long Beach municipal water. Install or replace is conditional and low emphasis.

Long Beach households are on a municipal system described by the utility, not on private wells. A well request may be a wrong address, a rural property outside this map, or a misunderstanding of an irrigation pump. Ask the caller where the well is. If they are on city water, stop and use the pipe guide. This site will not help locate drilling contractors.

Well Pump - Repair or Service

Availability has to be confirmed

Repair of a well pump is the same conditional bucket. Do not promise a visit.

If a property truly has a well, the provider has to accept that work and the electrical portion has to be clear. Most callers who reach a Long Beach plumbing referral do not have this problem. Saying so saves a wasted appointment.

Well Pumps Installed & Repaired

The three well labels are one conversation: confirm the property is not on municipal water, confirm the provider’s scope, and do not invent a local well market. Irrigation boosters and circulation pumps are not wells. Name the equipment you actually have.

Long Beach context for specialty work

The published hardness of the drinking water is the local fact that belongs on treatment. It supports a calm conversation about scale and softeners, including the sodium caution. It does not support a claim that tap water is unsafe, and it does not justify a well.

Backflow, sumps, manufactured homes, pools, radiant tubing, and wells each collide with a different authority: a water agency, a park, an electrician, or a building inspector. The permit center can speak to Long Beach building permits. It cannot certify a backflow tester. Nearby cities do not inherit Long Beach’s utility rules. Keep specialty requests address-specific, and keep them conditional until a provider says yes.

What is actually available

Which specialty items can I assume are available?

None. Treatment, backflow, sumps, manufactured homes, pool plumbing, hydronic radiant, and wells each have to be accepted on the call. Some are rare in Long Beach because the city is on municipal water and many houses have no sump. A section on this page means the topic is explained. It does not mean a provider is standing by.

Is a backflow test the same as a repair?

No. A test is a certified inspection and often a filing with a water supplier. A repair is work on the device. A company can do one, the other, both, or neither. Ask which one you need, and ask who files the result. This site does not claim to be a certified tester and does not state the city’s current form.

Do you handle pool cleaning or the pool pump’s wiring?

The only candidate scope is plumbing the provider agrees to touch. Chemical care, plaster, and electrical work are not included by this guide. A leak at the equipment pad is a different description from a leak in the shell. Say which you see. Acceptance of even the plumbing portion is unconfirmed.

We have a mobile home. What is different?

The coach, the space, and the park’s permission can be three different approvals. Under-floor access is often easier than in a slab house, and the sewer or water connection at the pad may not belong to the coach owner. Ask the park what it requires and ask the provider whether that park is in scope. Do not assume a residential ZIP rule covers the space.

Can a filter fix unsafe water?

This site will not call Long Beach tap water unsafe. The utility describes it as moderately hard to hard and tells filter owners to maintain the equipment. A softener adds sodium, which is a doctor question for some people. If you have a test result, take it to the provider and, for a health question, to a clinician or the utility.

Who is qualified for radiant heat or a well?

Only a provider who says, in the quote, that the work is inside their license and that they will do it. Hydronic radiant is not electric floor heat. A well is not a city meter. Most Long Beach referrals will never reach either job. If the provider declines, believe the decline. Permits and electrical work remain separate until a written scope includes them.
